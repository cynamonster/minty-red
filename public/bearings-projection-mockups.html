Why This Visual Cures Confusion

When players look at a flat schoolroom Mercator map, straight lines are rhumb lines (constant compass bearings). But on a sphere, lines of latitude are small circles, not straight shortest paths.

The Epiphany: The shortest path between any two points on a globe is always a great-circle slice through Earth's center. Notice how on the globe, the orange arc is the direct path, while the blue line spirals longer!
