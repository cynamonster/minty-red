💡 What Makes This Rotate in 2D Without 3D Engines?

Both globes use pure Orthographic Projection Mathematics: Every point $(\lambda, \phi)$ in 3D spherical space projects directly onto a 2D circle using the yaw $(\lambda_0)$ and pitch $(\phi_0)$ camera angles. When you drag, we simply increment $[\lambda_0, \phi_0]$. Any point where the angular distance from the camera center exceeds $90^\circ$ is geometrically hidden behind the sphere's horizon. This eliminates the need for 3D matrix transforms, WebGL shaders, or heavy engine bundles while keeping rendering instant and crisp!
